ABOUT ME

Hi, I am Tom

AI and ML engineer sharing 20+ years of experience - what works, what doesn't, and how to think clearly about it.

I am an experienced Data Scientist, Software Architect, Team Leader and Engineer with a strong focus on Machine Learning, AI, Analytics, Natural Language Processing and Big Data. My goal has always been the same: leverage the knowledge hidden in data. Discover. Never stop asking questions. Dig deeper.

I started working on AI before it was called that - and before it was popular. My footsteps trace through pure software development, business intelligence, analytics, data science, machine learning, NLP and LLMs. Over 20 years I have worked across start-ups and top-10 largest companies in the world, in academia, and on three continents in international environments.

I have seen a lot that worked and a lot that didn't. I have watched the same ideas fail, get rebranded, and fail again. This newsletter exists to share what I have actually learned - the experiences, the patterns, the things worth doing and the things worth avoiding - so you don't have to repeat the mistakes I've already made.

I write the way I would talk to you over coffee: direct, honest, and with enough depth to be genuinely useful. We probably won't have that coffee - but the newsletter is the next best thing.

Beyond the work

Away from data and code, I am a chess player - and have been for most of my life. Chess taught me a lot of what I apply at work: patience, the importance of thinking several moves ahead, and the humbling reality that confidence without calculation gets you into trouble.

I run a YouTube channel dedicated to chess - covering games, ideas, and the kind of thinking that makes the game endlessly interesting. If you play or are curious about the game, you are welcome to join that corner of the internet too.
